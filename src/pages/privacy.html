2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Newsletter and event updates: your email address.
  • Membership applications: your name, email address, city, what you do, and anything you choose to tell us about why you’d like to join.
  • Mentorship applications: your name, email address, industry, whether you want to find or become a mentor, and the goals you share.
  • Contact messages: your name, email address, the topic you choose and your message.
  • Member profiles and stories: the biography, job title, company, photo and other details you agree to have published.
  • Member account (when available): your login details and the profile information you add.

Please don’t send us sensitive information (such as health, financial account or government ID details) through our forms.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical information: like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you request and the date and time. This is used to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Your privacy choices: we store your cookie and privacy preferences in your browser so we don’t ask you on every page.
  • Analytics and marketing (only with your consent): if we add analytics or marketing tools, they will only run after you allow them in our privacy banner or Privacy settings. Our Cookie Policy lists any tools in use.

Information from others

If another member recommends you, introduces you or refers you to a mentor, they may give us your name and contact details. We use this only to make the introduction or follow up with you.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers who help us run Her Circle, such as website hosting, form processing, email delivery and event registration. They may use the information only to provide their services to us.
  • Mentors and mentees. When we make a mentorship introduction, we share the relevant parts of your application with your match.
  • Other members, only for information you choose to publish, such as a member profile or directory listing.
  • Legal and safety reasons, when required by law or to protect the rights, safety and property of our members, the public or Her Circle.
  • Organizational changes. If Her Circle is reorganized, merged or transferred, information may pass to the successor organization under the same protections described here.

5. Member profiles and stories

We publish a member’s name, biography, photo or story only with that member’s permission. You can ask us to update or remove your published profile at any time by contacting us. Copies of public pages may remain for a time in search engine caches or archives that we don’t control.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only essential browser storage by default. Analytics and marketing technologies run only if you allow them. Our fonts and images are hosted on our own site, so loading a page doesn’t send your information to font or image providers. See our Cookie Policy for details, and change your choices at any time in Privacy settings.

7. How long we keep information

  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.
  • Applications: for the length of your membership or mentorship, and up to 2 years after an application is declined or a membership ends.
  • Contact messages: up to 2 years after we last correspond.
  • Published profiles: until you ask us to remove them.
  • Privacy choices: stored in your browser for up to 12 months, after which we ask again.

We may keep information longer where the law requires it or to resolve disputes.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete your information;
  • receive your information in a portable format;
  • object to or restrict certain uses of your information;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting anything we did before you withdrew it.

You can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email we send. To make any other request, email {{site.contact_email}}. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the time required by law, usually 30 to 45 days. We won’t treat you differently for exercising your rights.

9. U.S. state privacy rights

Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah and other states with consumer privacy laws may have the rights described in section 8, as well as the right to opt out of the sale of personal information, sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising, targeted advertising and profiling. We do not sell or share personal information for these purposes. We also treat a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal from your browser as a request to opt out, and our site keeps marketing technologies off when it detects one.

In the last 12 months we have collected the categories of information described in section 2: identifiers (such as name and email address), professional information, internet activity on our site, and information you choose to share in messages and applications. We collect it for the purposes in section 3 and disclose it only to the recipients in section 4.

You may use an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf; we may ask the agent for proof of authorization. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision, and we will respond to your appeal within the time required by law.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and limiting access to people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. International transfers

Her Circle is based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States or other countries. Where the law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, when transferring personal information internationally.

13. Children

Her Circle is intended for adults. Our website is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
