1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in the same way. In this policy we call them all “cookies.”

2. What we use

Essential (always on)

These are needed for the site to work and don’t require consent.

Analytics (off unless you allow it)

Analytics help us understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are most read. We do not currently use any analytics tools. If we add one, we will list it here and it will only run after you allow analytics.

Marketing (off unless you allow it)

Marketing tools help us measure our outreach and show relevant content on other websites. We do not currently use any marketing tools. If we add one, we will list it here and it will only run after you allow marketing. A Global Privacy Control signal from your browser keeps marketing off.

3. Third-party content

Our fonts, images and scripts are hosted on our own website, so loading our pages does not send your information to font, image or analytics providers. If we embed third-party content in the future, such as videos or maps, we will update this policy.

4. Your choices

  • Open Privacy settings at any time to allow or turn off analytics and marketing. Settings are also linked in the footer of every page.
  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies and site data. Blocking essential storage means we can’t remember your choices, so you may see the privacy banner again.

5. Updates

When we change the cookies we use in a meaningful way, we will update this policy and ask for your choices again. Questions? Email {{site.contact_email}} or read our Privacy Policy.
