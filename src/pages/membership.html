FAQ

Questions, answered

Who can join Her Circle?

Her Circle centers Asian women and welcomes all women and non-binary people who share our values of connection, empowerment and inspiration.

Can I switch tiers later?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel anytime from your Member Portal account settings.

Are there scholarships?

Absolutely. We never want cost to be a barrier. Contact us and we’ll work with you on a sliding-scale membership.

Do you have chapters outside the US?

We currently have chapters in 12 cities across the US and Canada, and our virtual events welcome members everywhere.

How do I become a mentor?

Visit our mentorship page to apply. Mentors are welcome at any membership tier.
