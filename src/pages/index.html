Women helping women grow, lead and create impact.

Her Circle brings together founders, professionals and leaders to share real experience, learn from one another and open doors for the next woman.

Join Her Circle Our mission

Ambition grows faster in good company. We make sure no woman has to build alone.

Many of our members are building careers and companies in industries where they are still the only woman in the room. Her Circle is where they compare notes, find mentors and make the introductions that change what’s possible.

About Her Circle

Our four pillars

Everything we do comes back to four things.

  1. 01

    Connection

    Small gatherings and an online space where members get to know each other properly.

  2. 02

    Mentorship

    Pairing women who have built something with women who are building now.

  3. 03

    Leadership

    Practical sessions on negotiating, managing, raising money and being heard.

  4. 04

    Wellbeing

    Room to talk honestly about balance, family and the cost of ambition.
