Kristin Ma

Co-Founder, JXZ Flooring

Kristin Ma is a co-founder of JXZ Flooring, one of Ohio’s leading wholesale suppliers of engineered hardwood flooring. Her experience spans product development, international sourcing, supply-chain coordination, inventory strategy, wholesale distribution, and dealer network expansion across the Midwest.

As a woman entrepreneur building a business in the traditionally male-dominated flooring and construction materials industry, Kristin brings a distinctive combination of practical problem-solving, cross-cultural perspective, and relationship-driven leadership. She specializes in connecting global manufacturing capabilities with the evolving needs of the U.S. market and transforming complex operational challenges into sustainable business opportunities.

Kristin believes entrepreneurship is about more than growing a successful company—it is also about creating value, opening doors, and building communities where women can learn from and empower one another. Through the club, she looks forward to sharing real-world experience, learning from fellow founders, and helping more women turn ambition into meaningful and lasting impact.
