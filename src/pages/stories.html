Kristin Ma

Co-Founder, JXZ Flooring

Kristin Ma is a co-founder of JXZ Flooring, one of Ohio’s leading wholesale suppliers of engineered hardwood flooring. Her experience spans product development, international sourcing, supply-chain coordination, inventory strategy, wholesale distribution, and dealer network expansion across the Midwest.

As a woman entrepreneur building a business in the traditionally male-dominated flooring and construction materials industry, Kristin brings a distinctive combination of practical problem-solving, cross-cultural perspective, and relationship-driven leadership. She specializes in connecting global manufacturing capabilities with the evolving needs of the U.S. market and transforming complex operational challenges into sustainable business opportunities.

Kristin believes entrepreneurship is about more than growing a successful company—it is also about creating value, opening doors, and building communities where women can learn from and empower one another. Through the club, she looks forward to sharing real-world experience, learning from fellow founders, and helping more women turn ambition into meaningful and lasting impact.

  • Engineered hardwood flooring
  • International sourcing
  • Wholesale distribution
  • Ohio & the Midwest

Member profile

Kristen Kidder

Realtor and Real Estate Investor

Kristen Kidder is an experienced residential and commercial Realtor, representing both buyers and sellers throughout every stage of the real estate transaction. With six years of experience as a Realtor and real estate investor, she brings practical knowledge, strong negotiation skills, and an investor’s perspective to every client she serves.

As a real estate investor, Kristen is actively involved in multifamily properties, single-family rentals, property renovation, and mixed-use development. Before entering real estate, she gained 12 years of sales management experience in the IT industry. She is also a self-employed entrepreneur who has created and developed businesses across several industries, including technology, education, e-business, and real estate.

Kristen is always willing to try something new and approach challenges without fear. Outside of work, she is a passionate mountain climber who has reached an altitude of 7,206 meters. She is also a devoted nature and animal lover who believes in continuous growth, exploration, and meaningful connections.

  • Residential & commercial real estate
  • Multifamily & mixed-use investing
  • 12 years in IT sales management
  • Mountaineer, 7,206 m
