About Her Circle

A community where women learn from and empower one another.

Our story

Her Circle started with a simple idea: the women who have figured something out should make it easier for the women coming next.

Many of our members are building careers and companies in industries where they are still the only woman in the room. Her Circle is where they compare notes, ask the questions they can’t ask at work and make the introductions that change what’s possible.

Mission

To help women turn ambition into meaningful and lasting impact, through connection, mentorship and shared experience.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Build relationships that last longer than a business card exchange.

  2. 02

    Empower

    Share the practical knowledge, contacts and confidence to take the next step.

  3. 03

    Inspire

    Tell real stories that widen what women believe is possible for them.

Why we exist

Because no one should have to figure it out alone.

Talent is not what holds most women back. Access is: to mentors who have been there, to peers who will be honest, and to networks that open doors. Her Circle exists to make that access ordinary.
