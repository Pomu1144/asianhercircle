Our story

It started around a kitchen table

In 2019, our founder Grace Lin invited six women over for dinner. Each was successful on paper—and each admitted to feeling like the only one in the room at work. That night they made a promise: to meet every month and show up for each other.

Word spread. Six became sixty, then six hundred. Today Her Circle connects thousands of women across twelve cities and online, with the same spirit as that first dinner: warmth, honesty and fierce support.

Why Her Circle exists

Because representation isn’t enough on its own

Asian women are often celebrated as high performers yet remain underrepresented in leadership. Too many of us were raised to keep our heads down and let our work speak.

Her Circle exists to change that story—by giving women the community, mentors and confidence to speak up, step forward and lift others as they climb.
